MODULE 01

Lewis Structures

ஒரு molecule-இல் உள்ள valence electrons எவ்வாறு bonds மற்றும் lone pairs ஆக அமைந்துள்ளன என்பதைப் பார்ப்போம்.

Select a molecule

Total valence electrons HCN

Step-by-step explanation

7-step Lewis structure method

1Calculate the total number of valence electrons.
2Choose the central atom (usually the least electronegative atom, except H).
3Connect atoms using single bonds.
4Complete the octets of outer atoms.
5Place remaining electrons on the central atom.
6Form multiple bonds if the central atom lacks an octet.
7Check the total electron count and formal charges.

Electron pair repulsion

Electron regions repel one another and arrange themselves as far apart as possible.

Strongest repulsion.
2Lone pair – Bond pair

Multiple bonds count as one electron region in VSEPR.

Steric number = BP + LP

2 → sp | 3 → sp² | 4 → sp³ | 5 → sp³d | 6 → sp³d²
